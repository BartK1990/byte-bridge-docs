Protocol converter

A substation or plant with devices that speak different protocols, and systems that want the data in their own. One gateway on an industrial PC serves them all.

  • Map all points at once, or point by point with new addresses
  • Convert types, scale and offset on the way
  • Add calculated points such as totals or averages
  • SCADA commands are forwarded to the RTU, and its answer comes back
  • Runs as a Windows service that starts on boot
